Clavier: Voice Keyboard

Privacy policy · updated October 5, 2026

Home Support

Clavier has no network path. Nothing you dictate leaves your iPhone, nothing is stored on a server, and there is no analytics or advertising code in the binary. This policy is short because there is little to tell you.

Nothing is collected Your voice recordings, the text they become, and your dictation history live only on your device, inside Clavier's own private storage. They are deleted when you delete them, or when you delete the app.

How that works

The speech model runs entirely on your iPhone, in the Clavier app. When you dictate through the Clavier keyboard, the keyboard hands the recording to the app, the app transcribes it locally, and the finished text is placed in the field you were typing in — Notes, WhatsApp, Mail, anywhere. No step of that path touches a network.

What Clavier does not do

  • No telemetry, analytics, or crash reporting.
  • No advertising, ad identifiers, or third-party trackers.
  • No accounts, and no way for us to contact you through the app.
  • No selling, sharing, or processing of your data with anyone.

Data types and their destination

Voice audio and dictated text — created on-device, used on-device, discarded when you dismiss or delete a dictation. Destination: nowhere. This is the entire data flow.

Your rights

Because no data leaves your device, there is nothing for you to ask us to correct, export, or delete on a server: you already control all of it. To erase Clavier's local history, use the in-app reset, or delete the app.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the change will be announced in the app and this page will carry a new date. Given the architecture, we have no reason to change it.
